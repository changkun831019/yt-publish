常老师的发布工具

一个自用的命令行小工具：把我自己做好的视频传到我自己的 YouTube 频道，设好标题、说明、封面、播放列表和定时公开，再读回这些频道的播放数据。

它只在我自己的电脑上运行，只用在我自己的频道上，不对外提供，也不收集任何其他人的数据。

常老师的发布工具 (Chang's Publishing Tool)

A small command-line tool for my own use. It uploads videos I made to my own YouTube channels, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and scheduled publishing, and reads back view statistics for those channels.

It runs only on my own computer, is used only with my own channels, is not offered to anyone else, and collects no data about anyone else.
